# Employee Handbook

Kumo Systems is a fictional company used for this demo. This handbook applies to all employees.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as your team knows how to reach you. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours without the attendees' agreement.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on one shared office day so that people who sit together actually meet. Fully remote arrangements need approval from your manager and the People team. The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 50,000 yen for a desk, chair or monitor.

## Annual leave policy

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, plus national public holidays. Leave accrues monthly from your start date. You can carry over up to 5 unused leave days into the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March. Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick leave

You have 10 days of paid sick leave per year, separate from annual leave. Tell your manager as early as possible on the day. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken any time in the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, the People team, or the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a disciplinary offence.

## Information security basics

Lock your screen when you step away. Use the company password manager and enable multi-factor authentication on every work account. Never share customer data in public channels. Report lost devices or suspected phishing to the security team immediately at security@kumo.example.
