# Performance Review Guide for HR Partners

Confidential. HR use only.

## Review cycle

Performance reviews run twice a year, in April and October. Managers write reviews in the first two weeks of the cycle, calibration meetings take place in week three, and results are shared with employees in week four.

## Rating scale

Ratings use a five-point scale: 1 Below expectations, 2 Partially meets, 3 Meets expectations, 4 Exceeds expectations, 5 Outstanding. Calibration targets roughly 15% of employees at 4 or 5 and no more than 10% at 1 or 2.

## Calibration meetings

HR partners facilitate calibration meetings with all managers in a department. Each manager presents their proposed ratings with evidence. HR checks for rating inflation, bias and consistency across teams before ratings are final.

## Performance improvement plans

An employee rated 1 for a review cycle is placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan. The plan lists specific goals, weekly check-ins and the support offered. HR must review every plan before it is shared with the employee.

## Pay and promotion

Merit increases are tied to ratings: rating 3 receives a 3% raise, rating 4 receives 5%, and rating 5 receives 8%. Promotions are decided in the April cycle and take effect on 1 July.
