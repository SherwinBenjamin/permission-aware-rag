# Expense Policy

This policy covers business expenses for all employees.

## What you can claim

You can claim reasonable costs you incur for work: travel to customer sites, conference tickets approved by your manager, work books, and meals while travelling. Personal entertainment, fines and alcohol outside approved team events cannot be claimed.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Hotel rooms are capped at 20,000 yen per night in Tokyo and 15,000 yen per night elsewhere in Japan. Use public transport or shared rides where practical; taxis are fine late at night or when carrying equipment.

## Meals

While travelling on business, you can claim up to 5,000 yen per day for meals. Team dinners need prior approval from your manager and are capped at 6,000 yen per person.

## Submitting a claim

Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense, with a photo of the itemised receipt. Claims over 50,000 yen need your manager's approval before the purchase. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## Company cards

Managers and frequent travellers can request a company card. Card holders must reconcile every transaction in the finance portal by the fifth working day of the following month.
