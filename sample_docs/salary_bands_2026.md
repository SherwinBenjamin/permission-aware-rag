# Salary Bands 2026

Confidential. HR use only.

These are the 2026 annual base salary bands at Kumo Systems, in Japanese yen. Offers should normally land between the minimum and the midpoint of the band. Going above the midpoint requires approval from the VP of the department and HR.

## Engineering salary bands

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Junior Engineer | 4,800,000 | 5,600,000 | 6,400,000 |
| E2 | Engineer | 6,000,000 | 7,200,000 | 8,400,000 |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | 8,000,000 | 9,500,000 | 11,000,000 |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | 10,500,000 | 12,500,000 | 14,500,000 |
| E5 | Principal Engineer | 13,500,000 | 16,000,000 | 18,500,000 |

The senior engineer band (E3) runs from 8,000,000 to 11,000,000 yen with a midpoint of 9,500,000 yen.

## Sales and operations salary bands

| Level | Title | Minimum | Midpoint | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Associate | 4,200,000 | 4,900,000 | 5,600,000 |
| S2 | Specialist | 5,400,000 | 6,300,000 | 7,200,000 |

## Annual adjustment

Bands were raised 4% compared with 2025. Employees below the minimum of their band after the adjustment are raised to the minimum on 1 April 2026.
